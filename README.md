# Awesome-Food-Beverage-CRM

# Top Food & Beverage CRM Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Restaurant Operations, Guest Engagement & Back-of-House Automation*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Food & Beverage CRM**. These tools enable restaurants, hospitality groups, and food distributors to manage guest relationships, streamline operations, automate back-office tasks, and drive repeat visits through data-driven engagement.

**Examples** include Salesforce Food and Beverage Cloud, Toast POS, SevenRooms, TouchBistro, BentoBox, Upserve, Revel Systems, OpenTable, Lightspeed Restaurant, and MarginEdge (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom restaurant management, and open guest CRM — ideal for independent operators, hospitality groups, and developers seeking transparent, vendor-independent F&B solutions.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Food and Beverage Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/)**  
  AI-powered CRM platform for F&B manufacturers, distributors, and hospitality groups. Features demand forecasting, supply chain visibility, farm-to-fork traceability, and personalized customer engagement . Einstein AI predicts demand and identifies high-value leads, while Field Service Lightning optimizes route planning for field sales teams .

- **[Toast POS](https://pos.toasttab.com/)**  
  All-in-one cloud-based restaurant POS platform serving single locations to multi-unit groups. Features integrated payments, kitchen display systems, tableside ordering, online ordering, and guest engagement tools. Toast Tables brings reservations, waitlist, and table management under one roof . 24/7 support from hospitality-focused teams.

- **[SevenRooms](https://sevenrooms.com/)**  
  Hospitality CRM and operations platform focused on guest relationships. Features AI-powered table management, automated marketing (email/SMS), guest profiles with auto-tags, and white-labeled reservation/ordering systems. Connects to major booking channels including Google, Instagram, and Yelp . Venues report cutting third-party fees by 50% while gaining real-time guest data .

- **[TouchBistro](https://www.touchbistro.com/)**  
  iPad-based all-in-one POS and restaurant management system. Features tableside ordering, floor plan management, menu management, reservations with SMS reminders, loyalty program with CRM, and integrated marketing. Starts at $69/month for software; Essentials Bundle at $99/month with $0 upfront hardware . Works offline during connectivity loss.

- **[BentoBox](https://getbento.com/)**  
  Website platform and online ordering solution for restaurants. Features Takeout & Delivery with menu hours, delivery zones, group ordering, QR code order & pay, and digital gift cards redeemable during checkout. Configurable notification groups via email or phone .

- **[Upserve](https://upserve.com/)**  
  Restaurant management platform with POS, inventory, and analytics. Purpose-built workflows for changing menus, tracking inventory, and adjusting table layouts. Cloud-based with offline mode for uninterrupted service .

- **[Revel Systems](https://revelsystems.com/)**  
  Feature-rich, customizable POS for small, mid-sized, and multi-location businesses. Features near real-time reporting, loyalty and CRM customization, inventory management, and open API. PCI compliant with industry-leading fraud protection .

- **[OpenTable](https://www.opentable.com/)**  
  Guest relationship management platform with the world's largest diner network. Features comprehensive guest profiles (favorite dishes, seating preferences, spend history, social profiles), automated marketing, and AI-powered tools. OpenTable Pro adds customizable email campaigns, memberships, and ad tracking .

- **[Lightspeed Restaurant](https://www.lightspeedhq.com/)**  
  Cloud-based POS and restaurant management platform serving 146K+ locations globally including 200+ Michelin-starred restaurants. Features tableside ordering, QR code ordering, multi-location management with shared customer data, offline mode, and PMS integration for hotels. Reported 40% faster than other leading POS systems .

- **[MarginEdge](https://www.marginedge.com/)**  
  Restaurant management software focused on food cost and back-office automation. Features automated invoice processing (photos, email, EDI), real-time plate costs, daily controllable P&L, theoretical vs. actual usage reporting, menu engineering, and unlimited bill pay. Connects with 60+ POS systems and all major accounting platforms .

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[URY](https://github.com/navariltd/ury)**  
  FOSS restaurant management system built on ERPNext (license varies). Successfully running at scale across 10+ outlets for over 10 months . Features POS with dine-in/takeaway/delivery/offline modes, Kitchen Display System (URY MOSAIC) with multi-kitchen printer routing, table order management with real-time inventory checks, menu/recipe management with Bill of Materials, operational red flag alerts, and daily P&L reporting. Role-based access with shift management and cash reconciliation.

- **[MnemoResto](https://github.com/Mnemosyne-OS/MnemoResto---MnemosyneOS)**  
  Offline-first restaurant management cartridge for Mnemosyne OS (license varies) . Features interactive floor plan with live table states, reservations with iPhone-style date/time picker, guest CRM with loyalty and preference memory, multi-table POS with combos and per-product VAT, staff/tip management, menu and inventory tracking, and built-in AI copilot for service summaries. Trilingual interface (EN/FR/ES). All data stays local — intelligence comes to the data, not the other way around.

- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Comprehensive open-source ERP (GPL-3.0) with restaurant and hospitality modules. Features POS, inventory, accounting, CRM, and manufacturing. Can be extended for food service operations. URY is built on top of ERPNext, demonstrating its adaptability .

- **[Odoo](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Open-source business suite (LGPL-3.0) with POS, restaurant, and inventory modules. Features table management, kitchen printing, and multi-location support. Extensible with community modules for specific F&B workflows.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[OpenBoxes](https://github.com/openboxes/openboxes)** — Open-source supply chain and inventory management (EPL-1.0) for food distribution and logistics.
- **[Grocy](https://github.com/grocy/grocy)** — Open-source household/grocery management (MIT) adaptable for small retail inventory.
- **[Open Source POS](https://github.com/opensourcepos/opensourcepos)** — Open-source point of sale (MIT) for retail and quick-service transactions.
- **[Akeneo PIM](https://github.com/akeneo/pim-community-dev)** — Open-source Product Information Management (OSL-3.0) for managing complex F&B product catalogs.
- Community **n8n** workflows for restaurant automation and guest communication routing.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **URY** or **MnemoResto** as the core restaurant management platform with **ERPNext** for ERP integration, **Grocy** for inventory tracking, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade F&B CRM platform.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- F&B CRM platforms handling guest data must comply with data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA, PCI-DSS for payment data, etc.).
- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, payment gateway configuration, and ongoing maintenance for production reliability. Restaurant environments require hardware rated for heat, spills, and high-volume service conditions.
